Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026. Lyceum ("the app") is an independent study tool.

What we store

Your name, the material you add, your study record, answers, grades and signed contract are stored on your device (and in your device backup). We do not run accounts and do not receive this data unless a feature below sends it.

What leaves your device

  • Faculty features (generated papers, grading, lecture notes): the relevant course material and your answers are sent to Lyceum's server and forwarded to a language-model provider (OpenRouter and the model you or we select) to produce the result. They are not used to train models by us; provider terms apply.
  • Purchases: the store receipt is sent to our server to verify it.
  • Certificates: if you register a certificate for public verification, its number, your name, the course, the grade and a hash are stored on our server and shown at the verification link.
  • Web page import: the address you enter is fetched through a reader service.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps, no sale of data.

Your controls

Export or erase everything from More in the app. To remove a registered certificate, contact us with its number.
